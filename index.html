## Presale (first 333 PAID mints @ 0.0001 BNB flat)

- The first 333 **paid** mints cost a flat **0.0001 BNB** each, instead of
  the ladder price. The free mint (#1 per wallet) still runs during the
  presale and never counts toward the 333.
- `presaleSold` counts **paid mints only**; the presale closes itself at
  333 — no owner flip, no allowlist. After it closes, paid mints return
  to the per-wallet ladder.
- A batch may **straddle the boundary**: each mint in the batch is priced
  in order, so a batch can contain presale-priced and ladder-priced
  mints. Fresh wallet, qty 2 during the presale = 0 + 0.0001 =
  **0.0001 BNB**. At presaleSold = 332, a fresh wallet minting 3 paid
  (batch of 4 incl. free) pays 0 + 0.0001 + 0.001 + 0.002.
- Always read the exact batch cost from `priceFor(wallet, qty)` on-chain
  — never compute it locally, never use price × qty.

## Price ladder (ALL mints via SPACE ID Agent ID; free = any Agent ID holder, NO allowlist; applies to paid mints AFTER the presale)

|---|---|

- **Max 6 per wallet** (1 free + 5 paid). **Max 0.0115 BNB per wallet.**
- After the presale, a batch costs the **ladder sum** of the next prices
  — NEVER price × qty. Fresh wallet, qty 2 = 0 + 0.0005 = **0.0005 BNB**.
  Fresh wallet, qty 6 = **0.0115 BNB**. Wallet with 1 mint, qty 2 =
  0.0005 + 0.001 = **0.0015 BNB**.
- Minting is one transaction: `mint(uint256 qty)` mints the next sequential
  IDs and charges the exact `priceFor(wallet, qty)`. The exact value comes
  from `priceFor(wallet, qty)` read on-chain — always use that value, never
  a locally computed one, when sending the transaction.

## Marketplace agent prompts (`market.html`, agent-only, no wallet button)

Marketplace `0x5b6D7F4D89262a025ffdBd518e37FEF37316dd28` (PREDICTED).
Escrow model: `list()` moves the NFT into the marketplace; `delist()`
returns it; `buy()` pays the exact listed price — seller 95%, royalty 5%,
marketplace fee 0%. No floor price.

- **List:** verify `ownerOf(TOKEN_ID)` = MY_WALLET and `listing(TOKEN_ID)`
  not active; approve the marketplace (`approve` / `setApprovalForAll`);
  call `list(TOKEN_ID, PRICE_IN_WEI)` with price > 0.
- **Buy:** read `listing(TOKEN_ID)` (seller, price, active); if not
  active, STOP; call `buy(TOKEN_ID)` sending EXACTLY the listed price in
  BNB, native BNB only.
- **Delist / reprice:** read `listing(TOKEN_ID)`; seller must be
  MY_WALLET; call `delist(TOKEN_ID)` or `updatePrice(TOKEN_ID,
  NEW_PRICE_IN_WEI)` (price > 0).

Frequently asked questions

What is VANDA?

A 3,333-piece NFT collection of orchid specimens on BNB Chain (BEP-721). Five varieties — Vanda Alba, Vanda Rosea, Vanda Coerulea, Vanda Aurea and Miss Joaquim — each tied to a fixed Accession number range. The artwork is original, made for this collection.

What do I receive?

A BEP-721 token with sequential Accession Nº 001–3,333, its artwork and on-chain metadata. Paid mints also accrue 120,000 $VANDA each (max 600,000 per wallet).

Are the IDs random?

No — and that is deliberate. IDs are sequential in mint order: the first mint is Nº 001, the second Nº 002, and so on. Your variety follows your number, so there is no draw, no reveal and no luck involved. The last 84 mints (Nº 3,250–3,333) are the Legendary Miss Joaquim.

Is this affiliated with Binance?

No. VANDA is an independent project on BNB Chain, not a Binance collaboration. Minting uses a SPACE ID Agent ID as a gate, which is a requirement of the contract, not a partnership.

How much does it cost?

Mint #1 is FREE for any SPACE ID Agent ID holder. The first 333 paid mints cost a flat 0.0001 BNB (presale). After the presale, #2 is 0.0005, #3 is 0.001, #4 is 0.002, #5 is 0.003 and #6 is 0.005 BNB. One wallet can mint at most 6, spending at most 0.0115 BNB in total. A batch costs the exact on-chain price (priceFor) — never price × quantity — and a batch that crosses the presale boundary mixes presale and ladder prices.

What is the presale?

The first 333 paid mints cost 0.0001 BNB flat instead of the ladder price. The free first mint still runs during the presale, and free mints never count toward the 333. The presale closes itself when 333 paid mints are sold — there is no owner switch and no allowlist.

How do I mint without a wallet button?

There is no wallet button on this page by design. Copy the free-mint or paid/batch agent prompt from the mint panel, give it to your agent together with how many you want, and confirm in your agent. Your agent calls the contract directly; this page only reads public on-chain data.

How many can one wallet mint?

At most 6 VANDA in total, including the free one. The limit is enforced by the contract and counts every mint from that wallet.

Where is the official X account?

On X as @vanda_nfth — https://x.com/vanda_nfth. That handle and link are the only official X details published here.
