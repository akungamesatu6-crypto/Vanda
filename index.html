How it works

From stone to bloom

Minting is simple, and it is done by your agent or your wallet — never by us on your behalf.

1 · Hold a SPACE ID Agent ID

Every mint — free and paid — goes through a SPACE ID Agent ID. Your first mint is free; check your Agent ID below before you mint.

2 · Pick how many, 1–6

One wallet can hold at most 6 VANDA in total. Your first is free, then 0.0005, 0.001, 0.002, 0.003 and 0.005 BNB — at most 0.0115 BNB per wallet.

3 · Mint in one transaction

One batch mint gives you the next sequential Accession numbers in mint order. Paid mints each earn 120,000 $VANDA.

4 · The bloom is yours

Your NFT is a BEP-721 token on BNB Chain with its artwork and metadata at vanda-five.vercel.app/metadata/. Trade it on the VANDA marketplace — 0% fee.

Journal

Honest notes

What is VANDA?

A 3,333-piece NFT collection of orchid specimens on BNB Chain (BEP-721). Five varieties — Vanda Alba, Vanda Rosea, Vanda Coerulea, Vanda Aurea and Miss Joaquim — each tied to a fixed Accession number range. The artwork is original, made for this collection.

What do I receive?

A BEP-721 token with sequential Accession Nº 001–3,333, its artwork and on-chain metadata. Paid mints also accrue 120,000 $VANDA each (max 600,000 per wallet).

Are the IDs random?

No — and that is deliberate. IDs are sequential in mint order: the first mint is Nº 001, the second Nº 002, and so on. Your variety follows your number, so there is no draw, no reveal and no luck involved. The last 84 mints (Nº 3,250–3,333) are the Legendary Miss Joaquim.

Is this affiliated with Binance?

No. VANDA is an independent project on BNB Chain, not a Binance collaboration. Minting uses a SPACE ID Agent ID as a gate, which is a requirement of the contract, not a partnership.

How much does it cost?

Mint #1 is FREE for any SPACE ID Agent ID holder. Then #2 is 0.0005, #3 is 0.001, #4 is 0.002, #5 is 0.003 and #6 is 0.005 BNB. One wallet can mint at most 6, spending at most 0.0115 BNB in total. A batch costs the sum of the next ladder prices — never price × quantity.

How many can one wallet mint?

At most 6 VANDA in total, including the free one. The limit is enforced by the contract and counts every mint from that wallet.

Where is the official X account?

Not announced yet — the official X account is coming soon. There is no official handle right now; do not trust any account claiming to be VANDA until it is announced here.
