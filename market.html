How it works

Escrow, in three moves

The marketplace holds the NFT while it is listed. A seller cannot move or re-list it elsewhere in the meantime, and a buy can never fail on a missing approval.

1 · List

Your agent approves the marketplace for your token, then lists it at your price in BNB. The NFT moves into escrow — it stays there until it sells or you cancel.

2 · Buy

A buyer’s agent pays the exact listed price in BNB. The NFT moves to the buyer in the same transaction; the seller receives 95% and the 5% royalty is forwarded automatically.

3 · Delist

Change your mind? Your agent delists, and the NFT returns to your wallet. You can also update the price while it is listed.

Fees

Marketplace fee: 0%. Royalty: 5% to the collection owner (0x7379CA652f68fd1daAc11b7b87bb6B222fD129fa). No floor price, no other fees, ever. Payment must equal the listed price exactly.

Agent prompt — list a VANDA
You are my trading agent for the VANDA NFT collection on BNB Chain (BSC mainnet, chain ID 56).

MARKETPLACE CONTRACT (PREDICTED address — verify on BscScan that contract code exists at this address before trading; if it has no code yet, STOP and tell me the contracts are not deployed):
0x5b6D7F4D89262a025ffdBd518e37FEF37316dd28
NFT CONTRACT (PREDICTED): 0x0C4E3e87eB1194706822Cc7074B4c229F1da0419

I want to LIST Accession Nº TOKEN_ID at PRICE BNB.
Before listing, you MUST call these read-only functions and report the exact results:
1. ownerOf(TOKEN_ID) on the NFT contract — it must be MY_WALLET; if not, STOP.
2. listing(TOKEN_ID) on the marketplace — if it is already active, STOP and tell me.

Then ask me to confirm the token ID and price. Only after confirmation:
1. Approve the marketplace for this token (approve(marketplace, TOKEN_ID) on the NFT, or setApprovalForAll(marketplace, true)).
2. Call list(TOKEN_ID, PRICE_IN_WEI) on the marketplace. The price must be greater than 0.
The NFT moves into marketplace escrow until it sells or I delist it. After the transaction, report the transaction hash.
